# Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

<details>

<summary>Where can I use MYMI?</summary>

Use MYMI in a web browser on a computer or phone, or in the Android and iOS apps. The website is [https://www.mymi.live](https://www.mymi.live). See [Access MYMI](/getting-started/how-to-access.md) to get the app.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What are Sparks?</summary>

Sparks are what you spend to chat with the characters in a story. Each message uses Sparks based on the AI model you choose and the length of the reply. Buy them, or earn them through Attendance Check, Invite Friends, and events. See [Buying Sparks](/payment/payment-methods.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>It says I do not have enough Sparks</summary>

Buy more from **Profile → My Spark → Charge Sparks**. The notice in the chat can also take you to the purchase screen. You can earn free Sparks every day with **Attendance Check**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I want to cancel a purchase</summary>

For website purchases, contact MYMI. For app purchases, contact the App Store or Google Play. See [Cancellations and refunds](/payment/refund.md) for details.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I turn off the Safety Filter?</summary>

Only accounts that have completed **age confirmation** (18 or older) can turn off the filter. Turning off the **Safety Filter** on Home, or turning on **Show Sensitive Content** under **Profile → Settings & Support**, opens the age confirmation prompt. Phone verification alone does not turn off the filter. See [Age confirmation and phone verification](/account/adult-verification.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I change how chats are displayed?</summary>

Select the **palette icon → UI Settings** at the top right of the chat, then choose **Chat / 1:1 / Novel** under **UI Mode**. Each mode shows the same conversation history in a different way. See [Chat room settings](/chatting/chat-room-settings.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>A character does not remember earlier parts of the chat</summary>

An AI model can only refer to a limited amount of text at once, so it may miss earlier parts of a long conversation. Try the following:

1. In the chat room, open **⋮ → Memory Management → Memory Fragments** and check that **Use Memory Fragments** is on.
2. Open **⋮ → Memory Management → Long-term Memory** to review the summary of the conversation.
3. Write relationships or details that must not be forgotten in **User Note** (up to 2,000 characters) under **⋮ → Chat Settings**, then save.

See [Long-term memory & memory fragments](/chatting/memory-book.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>A chat room disappeared</summary>

A chat room may not appear in your list in these cases:

1. If you hid the room, it is in the hidden chats list under **View Previous Chats**.
2. The creator deleted the story, or it was removed for violating the rules.
3. Chat rooms for adult stories may be hidden depending on your age confirmation status.

If none of these apply, contact us with the story name, when the room disappeared, and your device and operating system.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How are AI stories reviewed?</summary>

Stories are reviewed under the Work Creation Policy and community guidelines. See [Terms and policies](/policy/terms.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What are Leaves?</summary>

Leaves are rewards for creators. Once you become a creator, you earn Leaves when other users spend paid Sparks chatting in your public works, and you can convert them into Sparks. See [Support creators](/creator-support/support.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I contact support?</summary>

Use **Profile → Discord → Contact Us** or email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live). Include your MYMI ID, the screen where the problem happened, and whether you are using the web, Android, or iOS.

</details>
