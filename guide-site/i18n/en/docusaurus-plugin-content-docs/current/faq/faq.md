# Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Find the situation you're stuck in and open it. You can also search this page for the message you see on your screen. If it isn't solved here, [contact us](#contact-us) at the bottom.

## Logging in and signing up

<details>

<summary>I can't log in</summary>

1. If you opened the link inside another app such as Instagram or Facebook, logging in may be blocked. Open [mymi.live](https://www.mymi.live) directly in Chrome or Safari and log in.
2. Make sure you used the social account you first signed up with. Each Google, X, or Apple account has its own MYMI account.
3. The app doesn't have X login, so if you signed up with X, log in on the website. If you signed up with Apple, you can't log in on the Android app, so log in on the website or on an iPhone.
4. If it still doesn't work, clear MYMI's site data in your browser and log in again. See [Clear cookies](/faq/cookie-clear.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I can't see my earlier chats or Sparks</summary>

Logging in with a different social account creates a new MYMI account. Select **Profile → Settings & Support → Logout**, then log in again with the social account you first signed up with.

</details>

<details>

<summary>It says "You cannot rejoin within 30 days of account deletion."</summary>

A social account you deleted can sign up again only 30 days after you deleted it. Even if you sign up again, the chats and Sparks from the deleted account don't come back. See [Account deletion](/account/delete-account.md) for details.

</details>

<details>

<summary>The screen asks for Korean identity verification, or my account is for the wrong country</summary>

The language MYMI is shown in when you sign up sets your account's country, and you can't change it afterward. If your account was created while MYMI was shown in Korean, it uses Korean identity verification. Contact us (see [Contact us](#contact-us)). See [Sign up](/account/sign-up.md) for how to sign up in English.

</details>

## Verification and adult works

<details>

<summary>Where do I verify my phone number or confirm my age?</summary>

- **Phone verification**: Select **Check-in and get Sparks** in **Profile → Attendance Check**. If you haven't verified yet, the **Phone Verification** window opens.
- **Age confirmation**: When you turn off the Safety Filter on Home, or turn on **Show Sensitive Content** in **Profile → Settings & Support**, the **Age Confirmation** window opens.

They are two separate checks. The steps are in [Age confirmation and phone verification](/account/adult-verification.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I want to turn off the Safety Filter / adult works don't show up</summary>

1. The shield-shaped switch at the right end of the **Recommended**, **Ranking**, **Tags**, and **Search** tab row on Home is the Safety Filter. While it's on (green), adult works are hidden.
2. Tap the switch to turn it off. If you haven't confirmed your age, the **Age Confirmation** window appears first. Check **I confirm that I am 18 years of age or older.** only if that's true, and select **Confirm**. Then check that the switch turned gray.
3. Works classified as sensitive content appear only when **Profile → Settings & Support → Show Sensitive Content** is on. It's a separate setting from the Safety Filter.

</details>

<details>

<summary>It says "This number is already linked to another account." or "A recently unlinked number can be registered again after 30 days."</summary>

- **If the number is already linked to another account**: A phone number can be linked to only one account. You probably verified it before on an account made with another social account. Log in with that account.
- **If the number was recently unlinked**: The number was on an account that was deleted or otherwise unlinked recently. You can register it again 30 days later.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I can't verify my phone number</summary>

Only mobile numbers from the United States, Japan, and Taiwan can be used. Check that the country selected in the **Phone Verification** window matches your number. If it doesn't, you see "Please enter a valid phone number." Numbers from other countries, including Canada, can't be used.

</details>

## Sparks and payments

<details>

<summary>It says "Insufficient credits. Please recharge your credits."</summary>

1. Select ⋮ at the top right of the chat room. Your remaining Sparks are at the top of the menu.
2. If you set **Response Length** above the default, that amount is deducted first when you send. If your remaining Sparks are fewer than that, you can type but the message won't send. Lower **Response Length** in the ⋮ menu and you can send.
3. If you're short on Sparks, buy some or get free ones. See [Buying Sparks](/payment/payment-methods.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I paid, but my Sparks didn't arrive</summary>

Check these before you pay again.

1. On the website, refresh the purchase screen. In the app, close it completely and open it again.
2. Open the **Recharge** tab in **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History** and look for the purchase.
3. If it's still not there, contact us with your payment details. What to send is in [If your Sparks did not arrive](/payment/refund.md#if-your-sparks-did-not-arrive).

The Android app rechecks unfinished payments and delivers Sparks if it finds one. When that happens, "Previous purchase restored" appears. If you see "There is a previous purchase that was not completed. Please try again shortly.", wait a moment and open the app again.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I want to cancel a payment or get a refund</summary>

If you paid on the website, ask MYMI. If you paid in the app, ask the App Store or Google Play. The conditions and steps are in [Cancellations and refunds](/payment/refund.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Attendance Check doesn't work</summary>

- Only accounts that have completed phone verification can get the reward. When you select **Check-in and get Sparks**, a verification window opens.
- You can get it once a day, and the day changes at 15:00 UTC. If you see "Already checked in today.", you can get the next one after the day changes.
- If you skip a day, your streak starts again from day 1.

The reward for each day is in [Buying Sparks](/payment/payment-methods.md#attendance-check).

</details>

<details>

<summary>My Invite Friends reward didn't arrive</summary>

The reward is given when your friend makes their first paid purchase, not when they enter the code.

1. Your friend has to complete phone verification **within 7 days** of signing up, then register the referral code in **Profile → Invite Friends**. If they signed up through your invite link, the code is registered automatically once they finish phone verification within 7 days.
2. If your friend makes their first paid purchase **within 30 days** of the day they register the code, you both get **1,500 Sparks**.
3. A purchase made before the code was registered doesn't count as the first purchase.

The full conditions are in [Buying Sparks](/payment/payment-methods.md#invite-friends).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I can't buy the First Buy package</summary>

- Only accounts that have completed phone verification can buy it. If you see "Purchase is only available for verified accounts.", verify first.
- You can buy it only once per account. If you see "This promotion has already been used.", you already bought it.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My Sparks went down</summary>

- Each kind of Spark lasts a different time. Sparks from Attendance Check and events expire after 14 days, Invite Friends rewards after 30 days, and purchased Sparks after 365 days.
- In the **Usage** tab of **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History**, you can see the Sparks you spent in chats and the Sparks that expired.

The periods are listed in [When do Sparks expire?](/payment/payment-methods.md#when-do-sparks-expire).

</details>

## Chatting

<details>

<summary>It says "Your free trial has ended" or "You've used all trial messages. Sign up to enjoy more conversations!"</summary>

Without logging in, you can chat up to **5 times**. Regenerate counts as one too. If you sign up right where you were trying it out, you can continue that chat on your new account. See [Sign up](/account/sign-up.md) for how to sign up.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I don't like the reply</summary>

Use the icons under the latest AI reply.

- Regenerate: get a new reply. It uses Sparks again. You can swipe to compare it with the earlier reply.
- Edit: change the reply yourself.
- Delete: the message of yours that asked for the reply is deleted too, and you can't undo it.

To change where the story goes, write what you want in **Chat Settings → User Note** in the chat room's ⋮ menu, or pick another model with the ⚡ button to the left of the message box.

</details>

<details>

<summary>A reply gets cut off in the middle</summary>

If you see "Max output limit reached", the reply stopped at its length limit. Select **Adjust** in the window and choose a larger value, and you'll get longer replies from the next one on. Longer replies use more Sparks. The cost for each model is in [Get longer replies](/chatting/ai-models.md#get-longer-replies).

</details>

<details>

<summary>A character doesn't remember our earlier chats</summary>

An AI model can only look at so much at once, so it can lose track of the early part of a long chat.

1. In the chat room's ⋮ menu, go to **Memory Management → Memory Fragments** and check that **Use Memory Fragments** (**Use Memory** in the app) is on.
2. In **Memory Management → Long-term Memory**, check how the story so far has been summed up.
3. Write relationships and settings that must be remembered in **Chat Settings → User Note** (up to 2,000 characters) and save.

More details are in [Long-term memory & memory fragments](/chatting/memory-book.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I can't edit or delete a message</summary>

- You can regenerate, edit, and delete only the latest AI reply.
- Once you save a bookmark, the bookmarked reply and the messages before it can't be edited or deleted anymore. Deleting the bookmark doesn't undo this. You see "This message is referenced by a branched chat and cannot be edited or deleted."

See [Bookmarks and branches](/chatting/chat-room-settings.md#bookmarks-and-branches) for bookmarks.

</details>

<details>

<summary>A chat room disappeared</summary>

The chat list shows only the most recent chat room for each work. Check these.

1. **If you started a new chat**: the earlier chat room is in **View Previous Chats** in that work's chat room ⋮ menu.
2. **If you hid the work in the chat list**: in **Chat** at the bottom, select **Hidden N** on the **Works** tab to open the hidden works. Then select **Unhide** in the work card's more menu.
3. **If you hid a single chat room**: in **View Previous Chats**, select the eye icon to open **Hidden chats**, choose the chat room, and select **Unhide**.

If you select **Delete** in the chat list, all chats of that work are deleted and you can't undo it.

</details>

<details>

<summary>It says "The model declined to respond"</summary>

If inappropriate content builds up in earlier chats, long-term memory, or memory fragments, the model can refuse even when the message you just sent is fine.

1. Select **Delete and continue** in the window, then rephrase or tone it down and send again.
2. For a declined reply, only 20% of the base price is deducted, and the rest is returned.
3. If it declined even though nothing inappropriate was there, [contact us](#contact-us).

</details>

<details>

<summary>It says "The selected model is temporarily unavailable. Please choose another model."</summary>

That model can't be used for a while. Pick another model with the ⚡ button to the left of the message box and send again. A model marked **Temporarily Disabled** in the model list can't be chosen right now. The **Smooth**, **Normal**, and **Busy** labels can help you choose.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Sparks were deducted, but no reply came</summary>

If a reply can't be made, the Sparks deducted up front are returned. You can see the returned Sparks in the **Recharge** tab of **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I want to change how the chat screen looks</summary>

Select the palette icon at the top right of the chat screen, then **UI Settings**, and choose **UI Mode** from **Chat / 1:1 / Novel**. Every mode shows the same chat history in a different way. More details are in [Chat room settings](/chatting/chat-room-settings.md).

</details>

## Creating works

<details>

<summary>It says "Identity verification is required to generate AI images."</summary>

You can't start verification on the creation screen.

1. Select **Save draft** at the top right to save your work.
2. Select **Check-in and get Sparks** in **Profile → Attendance Check** and complete phone verification.
3. Open the work again from **Profile → Content Management** and generate the image.

Image generation in the creation screen is free.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I can't finish creating my work</summary>

When you select **Publish**, you're taken to the tab with missing items. These are often missed.

- **Basic info**: Title, One-line description, Cover, Description, Genres, Hashtags
- **Prompt** and **Openings**: can't be empty
- **Settings**: you need to choose each of Publishing scope, Monetization limits, Image source, Audience, and Marketing consent

All the requirements are in [Limits and rules at a glance](/create/making/limits.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>My work is "Under Review" or "Rejected"</summary>

A newly published work goes through review before it goes public. If it's rejected, you can see the reason in **Profile → Content Management**, fix it, and ask for another review. The whole process is in [Publish and edit](/create/making/publish.md).

</details>

## Creators

<details>

<summary>I can't apply as a creator</summary>

You can apply when other users have had **5,000** or more chats in your works and you have **3** or more approved, public works. Chats you have in your own works don't count. You can see your progress in **Profile → Creator**. How to apply is in [Becoming a creator and Leaves](/creator-support/support.md#apply-as-a-creator).

</details>

<details>

<summary>My Leaves aren't adding up</summary>

Leaves are added after you become a creator, when other users chat in your approved, public works with paid Sparks. Chats with free Sparks from Attendance Check or events don't add Leaves. Depending on your work's settings, the rate differs, and some works earn none. The rates are in [Becoming a creator and Leaves](/creator-support/support.md#earn-leaves).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I turn Leaves into cash?</summary>

Not on an English account. Cash withdrawal isn't available for creators who don't live in Korea, and Leaves can only be converted into Sparks. Leaves expire after 30 days, so convert them in time. See [Becoming a creator and Leaves](/creator-support/support.md#convert-leaves-into-sparks).

</details>

## Contact us

If this page doesn't solve your problem, let us know.

- **Discord**: Join MYMI's Discord from **Profile → Discord**, and leave a message in the support channel.
- **Email**: [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live). Please email questions about payments and refunds.

Include your MYMI ID, the screen where the problem happened, and whether you use the website, Android, or iPhone, and we can check faster. In the app, **Profile → Settings & Support → Version Info → Copy support info** copies your environment details in one step.
