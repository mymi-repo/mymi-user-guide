# Clear cookies

If you keep getting logged out on the website, you see the login screen even after you log in, or **Profile** won't open, try these steps in order.

You don't need to clear cookies in the app. Select **Profile → Settings & Support → Logout**, then log in again with the social account you signed up with.

## Log in again first

If you can see the Logout button on **Profile**, select **Profile → Settings & Support → Logout**, then log in again with the social account you signed up with.

If you can't see the Logout button, or the same problem happens after you log in again, clear the cookies and site data for MYMI (mymi.live) in the browser you use. You need to log in again afterward.

## Clear your browser's cookies

The steps depend on your browser and its version, so follow your browser's official help. If you can delete the data of a single site, delete only mymi.live's data.

| Browser | Official help |
| --- | --- |
| Chrome (computer, Android, iPhone) | [Google Chrome Help: Delete and manage cookies](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en) |
| Safari (iPhone, iPad) | [Apple Support: Clear the history, cache, and cookies from Safari on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082) |
| Safari (Mac) | [Apple Support: Clear your cache and cookies in Safari on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac) |
| Microsoft Edge | [Microsoft Support: Delete cookies in Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09) |

:::warning[You may be logged out of other sites too]
Clearing all cookies can log you out of other sites, not only MYMI.
:::

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>I cleared the cookies, but I still can't log in</summary>

- If you opened MYMI inside another app such as Instagram or Facebook, open [www.mymi.live](https://www.mymi.live) directly in Chrome or Safari.
- Try logging in with another browser. If it works there, the problem may be a setting in the browser you were using.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I got logged out of other sites too</summary>

Clearing cookies for your whole browsing history also logs you out of other sites. Log in to those sites again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I logged in, but my earlier chats or Sparks are gone</summary>

This isn't a cookie problem. You may have logged in with a different social account, and each social account has its own MYMI account. Log out, then log in again with the social account you first signed up with.

</details>

If it's still not solved, tell us your device and browser through [Contact us](/faq/faq.md#contact-us).
