# Clear cookies

If Profile or Logout does not appear on the website, or verification does not go through, try these steps in order.

## 1. Sign in again

Select **Profile → Settings & Support → Logout**, then sign in again with the social account you used to sign up.

If that does not help, clear the cookies and site data for MYMI (mymi.live) in your browser.

## 2. Clear your browser's cookies

The steps depend on your browser and its version. See the official help for your browser. If you can delete data for one site, delete only the data for mymi.live.

| Browser | Official help |
| --- | --- |
| Chrome (computer, Android, iPhone) | [Google Chrome Help: delete and manage cookies](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en) |
| Safari (iPhone, iPad) | [Apple Support: clear history, cache, and cookies in Safari on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082) |
| Safari (Mac) | [Apple Support: clear cache and cookies in Safari on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac) |
| Microsoft Edge | [Microsoft Support: manage cookies in Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09) |

:::warning
Clearing all cookies may also sign you out of other websites.
:::

## Getting help

If the problem continues, use **Profile → Discord → Contact Us** or email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live). Include your device and browser to help us check.
