# Choose your work settings

These settings decide how your work goes public and what conditions you earn Leaves under. You choose them in **Settings**, and Simple mode and Expert mode use the same cards.

- Simple mode: the **Settings** step. **Genres** and **Hashtags** are at the top, and the cards on this page follow below them.
- Expert mode: the **Settings** tab. **Genres** and **Hashtags** are in the **Basic info** tab.

A card marked with a red `*` is required. **Visibility** and **Content rating** are already chosen when you start. You have to choose the rest yourself before the work can be completed.

## At a glance

| Card | Choices | Starts as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Visibility** | Public · Private | Public |
| **Publishing scope** | Original · General | Not chosen |
| **Monetization limits** | No limits · Limited | Not chosen |
| **Image source** | Made with MYMI · Made elsewhere | Not chosen |
| **Audience** | For men · For women | Not chosen |
| **Content rating** | All ages · Adults only | All ages |
| **Marketing consent** | Agree · Don't agree | Not chosen |

## Visibility

Decide who can see the work.

- **Public**: Everyone can see it. A new work you make public goes through review before other users see it.
- **Private**: Only you can see it. Use it to try out chats before you make the work public.

You can change it later in **Settings** when you open the work from **Profile → Content Management → Edit**. If you change a private work to public, review starts from that point. ([Publish and edit](/create/publish#make-a-private-work-public))

## Publishing scope

- **Original**: A work published only on MYMI. It earns a higher Leaf rate.
- **General**: A work that may also be published on other platforms.

:::warning[Original can't be undone for 3 months]
When you choose Original, a note appears: "Once set to Original, it can't be undone for 3 months." The rule is that you don't switch back to **General** for 3 months from the day you set it. If you plan to post the work on other platforms too, choose **General**.

In Simple mode, the card is locked for 3 months after you set it, and the days left until it unlocks are shown.
:::

## Monetization limits

- **No limits**: A work whose rights you hold.
- **Limited**: A work whose rights aren't yours, like a derivative work that uses another work's characters or setting.

A work set to **Limited** doesn't earn Leaves, even if it's an Original or the creator is an Official Creator. A change applies from the next chat.

## Image source

Choose how the work's images were made.

- **Made with MYMI**: Images made with MYMI's image generation.
- **Made elsewhere**: Images made with another AI or drawn by hand.

For a **General** work, the Leaf rate depends on the image source. The rates are in [Becoming a creator and Leaves](/creator-support/support#earn-leaves). If your work doesn't use images, choose the one that applies to what you actually use.

## Audience

Choose **For men** or **For women** for who the work is mainly aimed at.

## Content rating

This affects the conversation and the generated images.

- **All ages**: Anyone can use it.
- **Adults only**: Only users who have completed adult verification can use it. ([Age confirmation and phone verification](/account/adult-verification))

If you change an already published work's rating from Adults only to All ages, it goes through review again.

## Marketing consent

- **Agree**: MYMI may feature this work in marketing such as ads and social media. More exposure naturally means more conversations, and that can mean more Leaves.
- **Don't agree**: The work isn't used for marketing.

## A card only in Simple mode: Image Output Instructions

**Settings** in Simple mode has one more card, **Image Output Instructions**. It decides how the AI sends images in a chat.

- **Internal Images**: Uses the images you uploaded to Assets. It's chosen from the start.
- **External Images**: Uses the image addresses (URLs) you write in the settings.

Use just one. The details are in [Assets and AI images](/create/simple/assets#image-output-instructions). Expert mode doesn't have this card. You write the image rules in the prompt yourself.

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>I chose Original and now I want to change to General</summary>

The rule is that an Original work can't be undone for 3 months after you set it. In Simple mode the card is locked and shows the days left until it unlocks. After 3 months you can change it to **General**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I don't know whether to choose Monetization limits</summary>

If you wrote the work with characters and a world you created, choose **No limits**. If the rights aren't yours, like a derivative work that takes another work's characters or setting, choose **Limited**. If it's hard to tell, ask through [Contact us](/faq#contact-us).

</details>

<details>
<summary>A message like "Choose the publishing scope." appears</summary>

It means a card hasn't been chosen. Messages appear one at a time. Simple mode checks when you select the next button or **Done**, and Expert mode checks when you select **Publish**. Choose the card the message names. More cards may still be left.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I made an Adults only work and other users can't see it</summary>

An Adults only work is shown only to users who have completed adult verification. Also check that your own account is verified. ([Age confirmation and phone verification](/account/adult-verification))

</details>
