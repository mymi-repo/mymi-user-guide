# Sign up

Log in with Google, X, or Apple, then choose your name, ID, and nickname to finish signing up. Children under 14 need a parent or guardian's consent to use MYMI.

:::warning[Make sure MYMI is in English before you sign up]
The language MYMI is shown in when you sign up sets your account's country. It decides how you verify your phone and age and how you pay, and it can't be changed after you sign up.

- On the website, open [www.mymi.live/en](https://www.mymi.live/en).
- The app uses your phone's language when you first open it. If the app isn't in English, sign up on the website instead.
:::

## Choose a sign-in button

1. Open [www.mymi.live/en](https://www.mymi.live/en) or the MYMI app and select **Profile** in the bottom menu. You can also start from the login window that appears while chatting.
2. Select the button for the social account you want to sign up with. The buttons you see depend on where you use MYMI, as shown below.
3. When you finish signing in with that service, the MYMI **Sign Up** screen opens.

| Where | Sign-in buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Website (computer or phone) | **Continue with Google**, **Continue with X**, **Continue with Apple** |
| Android app | **Continue with Kakao**, **Continue with Google** |
| iPhone app | **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Kakao**, **Continue with Google** |

:::tip[Decide which social account to use first]
Each social account gets its own MYMI account. From then on, log in with the same social account to keep your chats and Sparks. If you plan to use the app, sign up with Google, or with Apple if you only use an iPhone. X sign-in works only on the website, and the Android app has no Apple sign-in.
:::

## Choose your name, ID, and nickname

| Field | What it's for | Input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Display Name** | The name characters call you | Up to 20 characters |
| **Gender setting** | Your gender, which characters take into account | **Female**, **Male**, or **Other** |
| **User ID** | An address like `@moon_reader` that identifies you | 3–20 characters. Follow the rules below |
| **Nickname** | The name other users see | Up to 30 characters |

For example, if your display name is `Haru` and your nickname is `MoonReader`, characters call you "Haru" and other users see you as "MoonReader." To use different names in different works, create more in [Persona settings](/account/persona.md) after you sign up. You can change your display name, ID, and nickname later in [Login and profile settings](/account/login-profile.md).

In the app, the **Display Name** field may already contain the name from your social account. You can change it to the name you want to be called in chats.

### ID rules

- Use only lowercase letters, numbers, underscores (`_`), and hyphens (`-`). Capital letters turn into lowercase, and other characters can't be typed.
- Use 3–20 characters.
- An ID can't start or end with `_` or `-`.
- `_` and `-` can't be next to each other. `__`, `--`, and `_-` aren't allowed.

| Example | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `moon_reader`, `reader-123` | Allowed |
| `ab` | Shorter than 3 characters |
| `_reader`, `reader-` | Starts or ends with `_` or `-` |
| `moon__reader`, `moon-_reader` | `_` and `-` are next to each other |

The result appears on the right of the field as you type your ID. If you see **Available**, move on. **Unavailable** means someone else uses that ID, so choose another one. **Invalid format** means it doesn't follow the rules above.

## Agree to the terms

| Item | Required? |
| --- | --- |
| **Agree to Terms of Service** | Required |
| **Agree to collection and use of personal information** | Required |
| **Service guidelines** | Required |
| **Agree to receive marketing information** | Optional |

- To agree, tap the **box to the left** of each item. Tapping the item's name opens its full text.
- **Agree to all** also checks the optional marketing item. If you don't want marketing messages, tap that box again to uncheck it.

## Finish signing up

The button at the bottom of the screen stays a gray arrow until everything is filled in. Fill in the four fields, make sure your ID shows **Available**, and check the three required items. The button then changes to **Complete Sign Up**. Select it to finish and go to Home.

You need Sparks to chat. After you verify your phone number, you can get them free every day from **Profile → Attendance Check**. See [Age confirmation and phone verification](/account/adult-verification.md) and [Buying Sparks](/payment/payment-methods.md#attendance-check).

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>The Complete Sign Up button is only a gray arrow</summary>

Something isn't filled in yet.

- Did you fill in Display Name, Gender setting, User ID, and Nickname?
- Does **Available** appear on the right of the User ID field?
- Did you check the three required items? Tap the box on the left, not the item's name.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Tapping an item opened the terms page</summary>

Tapping an item's name opens its full text. When you're done reading, go back to the sign-up screen and tap the box to the left of the name.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says "This ID is already taken."</summary>

Someone else uses that ID. Add numbers or `_` to try a different one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says "An error occurred while checking. Please try again."</summary>

Check your internet connection, then select **Check** on the right of the User ID field to check again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says "Rejoin Restricted" and "You cannot rejoin within 30 days of account deletion."</summary>

It's been less than 30 days since you deleted the MYMI account made with this social account. After 30 days, you can sign up again with the same social account, and you'll start with a new account. You can sign up with a different social account now, but the phone number verified on the deleted account can't be used again until 30 days after the deletion.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says "A problem occurred during sign up." or "A problem occurred during login."</summary>

Select **Please try again!** to go back to Home, then log in again from the beginning. If you opened MYMI inside an app like Instagram or Facebook, open [www.mymi.live/en](https://www.mymi.live/en) in Chrome or Safari and try again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I already signed up, but the Sign Up screen appears</summary>

You logged in with a different social account from the one you signed up with. Signing up here creates a new account, so go back and log in with the social account you first used. If you have several Google accounts, check that you chose the one you used for MYMI.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't log in to the app with X, or to the Android app with Apple</summary>

The app has no X sign-in, and the Android app has no Apple sign-in. Log in on the website instead: on your phone, open [www.mymi.live/en](https://www.mymi.live/en) in Chrome or Safari and select the button you signed up with.

</details>

If that doesn't solve it, reach us in the support channel on Discord from **Profile → Discord**, or email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) with the message on your screen.
