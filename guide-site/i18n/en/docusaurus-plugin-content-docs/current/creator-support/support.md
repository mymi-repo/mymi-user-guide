# Becoming a creator and Leaves

When other users chat a lot in your works, you can apply as a creator. As a creator, you earn a share of the paid Sparks spent in your works as **Leaves**, and you can convert Leaves into Sparks.

If you want to cheer on a creator you like, go to [Support a creator](#support-a-creator).

## Apply as a creator

In **Profile → Creator**, you can see how close you are to the requirements.

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Chat volume | At least 5,000 chats that other users had in your works. Chats you have in your own works don't count |
| Work count | At least 3 works that were approved and are public |

1. When **Complete** shows for both requirements, select the box to the left of **I agree to the Creator Terms**. Selecting the text opens the terms.
2. Select **Apply as Creator**.
3. If the requirements check out, you become a creator right away, and your Leaves and chat volume appear on the same screen.

The terms are in the [MYMI Creator Agreement](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/creator-terms).

## Earn Leaves

Once you're a creator, a share of the Sparks that other users spend when they chat in your works with **Paid Spark** is added to your Leaves. Only works that were approved and are public earn Leaves. Chats with free Sparks, and chats you have in your own works, don't earn them.

The rate depends on the **Publishing scope** and **Image source** you chose under **Settings** on the creation screen.

| Publishing scope | Image source | Leaf rate |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Original | Either | 5% |
| General | Made elsewhere | 3% |
| General | Made with MYMI | No Leaves |

For example, if another user spends 100 paid Sparks in your Original work, you earn 5 Leaves. Official Creators earn 7% in Original works.

Original works are published only on MYMI. Once you set a work to Original, you can't undo it for 3 months.

Works set to **Limited** under **Monetization limits** don't earn Leaves. This setting is for works whose rights aren't yours, such as derivative works that use another work's characters or setting. It applies even to Original works and to Official Creators. It takes effect from chats after you choose it, and Leaves you've already earned stay as they are.

:::warning[Leaves expire after 30 days]
Convert the Leaves you earn into Sparks within 30 days.
:::

## Convert Leaves into Sparks

1. In **Profile → Creator**, check **Current Leaves** and select **Convert to Spark**.
2. Enter the **Convert amount (units of 100)**. Select **All** to fill in as much as you can convert.
3. Check the Sparks you'll get, then select **Convert**.

| Level | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Creator | 100 Leaves = 80 Sparks |
| Official Creator | 100 Leaves = 100 Sparks |

- You can convert starting from 100 Leaves, in units of 100.
- It happens right away with no fee, and you can't undo it.
- The Sparks you get this way last for 1 year from the day you get them.

## See how your works are doing

Select **Profile → Content Management → Dashboard** to open the **Creator Dashboard**.

- See **Total Messages**, **My Messages**, and **Earned Leaves** at a glance.
- Switch the period to **Today** or **7d** to see daily trends and a ranking of popular works.
- Select a work in **Performance by Work** to see more detail.

The dashboard counts messages differently from the chat volume in the application requirements. Check the requirements in **Profile → Creator**.

## Become an Official Creator

When your chat volume reaches **50,000**, **Submit Application** appears in the **Apply for Official Creator** section of **Profile → Creator**. Select it to open the application form. The MYMI team reviews it, and you become an Official Creator once it's approved.

As an Official Creator:

- Original works earn Leaves at 7%.
- Chats you have in your own works earn Leaves too.
- 100 Leaves convert into 100 Sparks.

:::info[Leaves can't be turned into cash on English accounts]
Cash withdrawal isn't available for creators who don't live in Korea. Leaves can only be converted into Sparks. The Creator screen may show **Cash Withdrawal** with a limit in Korean won, but that doesn't apply to English accounts. The addendum of the [MYMI Creator Agreement](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/creator-terms) explains this.
:::

:::info[What Official Creators need to keep up]
Create at least 2 Original works a month, and register new works only as Original. Your status can be changed or revoked at MYMI's discretion. For details, see Article 5 of the [MYMI Creator Agreement](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/creator-terms).
:::

## Support a creator

Select a creator's name on a work's page to open their creator profile. You can see their works, **Announcements**, and **Comments**.

- Select **Follow** and the button changes to **Following**. You get a notification when they post a new work or announcement. Select **Following** again to unfollow.
- Leave a message of support in **Comments**. You can reply to other comments, and edit or delete your own.
- A **Secret comment** can be seen only by you and the creator. A secret reply can also be seen by the person you replied to.
- Creators can delete comments on their own profile. If a comment that started a thread is deleted, the replies under it disappear too.

You can see the creators you follow and the comments you wrote in the **Following** and **Comments** tabs of **Profile → My Activity**.

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>I can't select Apply as Creator</summary>

- **Complete** has to show for both requirements.
- To check **I agree to the Creator Terms**, select the box on the left, not the text.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I made more than 3 works, but my work count hasn't changed</summary>

Only works that were approved and are public count. Works under review, or works you made private, aren't counted. Even after a work is approved, the number updates a little later.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My Leaves aren't adding up</summary>

Leaves aren't added for these chats:

- Chats other users had with free Sparks
- Chats you had in your own works (Official Creators do earn Leaves for these)
- Works whose **Publishing scope** is General and whose **Image source** is Made with MYMI
- Works that are private or not approved yet

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't select Convert to Spark</summary>

If you see "Need 100+ Leaves", you have fewer than 100 Leaves. You can convert once you have 100.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Nothing happens when I select Cash Withdrawal</summary>

Cash withdrawal isn't available for accounts outside Korea. Convert your Leaves into Sparks instead.

</details>

For other problems, see [Contact us](/faq/faq.md#contact-us).
