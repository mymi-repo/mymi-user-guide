# Support creators

You can support the creators of stories you enjoy by following them and leaving comments. If you make your own works, you can apply to become a creator and earn **Leaves**.

## 1. View a creator's profile

Select the creator's name on a story's detail page to open their profile. There you can see their works, **Announcements**, and **Comments**.

## 2. Follow a creator

Select **Follow** on the profile to get notified when the creator publishes a new work. Select it again to unfollow. You can find the creators you follow under **Profile → My Activity → Following**.

## 3. Leave a comment

Leave a comment on the **Comments** tab of the creator's profile.

- You can edit or delete your own comments, and reply to other comments.
- A **Secret comment** is visible only to you and the creator. A secret reply is also visible to the person you replied to.
- Creators can delete comments on their own profile. When a comment is deleted, the replies under it are no longer shown.

You can find your comments under **Profile → My Activity → Comments**.

## 4. Apply to become a creator

Check your progress toward the requirements under **Profile → Creator**.

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Chat Volume | At least 5,000 chats by other users in your works |
| Work Count | At least 3 approved works |

1. Once you meet both requirements, select **I agree to the Creator Terms**.
2. Select **Apply as Creator**.
3. When the requirements are confirmed, you become a creator.

Your own chats in your works do not count toward Chat Volume. See the [MYMI Creator Agreement](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/creator-terms).

## 5. Earn Leaves

As a creator, you earn Leaves when other users spend **paid Sparks** chatting in your works that are public and approved. A share of the Sparks spent becomes Leaves. Chats that use free Sparks do not earn Leaves.

The rate depends on the **Publishing scope** and **Image source** you chose in the work's **Settings** tab.

| Publishing scope | Image source | Leaf rate |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Original | Any | 5% |
| General | Made elsewhere | 3% |
| General | Made with MYMI | None |

- Official Creators earn 7% on Original works. The other rates are the same.
- For example, if 100 paid Sparks are spent in an Original work, you earn 5 Leaves.
- Your own chats in your works do not earn Leaves. Official Creators also earn Leaves from their own chats.
- A work marked **Limited** under Monetization limits on the creation screen is described as a work that can't earn Leaves.
- Leaves must be used **within 30 days** of earning them. Convert them to Sparks before they expire.

## 6. Convert Leaves to Sparks

1. Under **Profile → Creator**, check **Current Leaves** and select **Convert to Spark**.
2. Enter the **Convert amount (units of 100)**. Select **All** to fill in the amount you can convert.
3. Check the Sparks you will receive, then select **Convert**.

| Tier | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Creator | 100 Leaves = 80 Sparks |
| Official Creator | 100 Leaves = 100 Sparks |

- You need at least 100 Leaves, and you can convert only in units of 100.
- Conversion is instant, has no fee, and cannot be undone.
- Sparks converted from Leaves can be used for 1 year from the date you receive them.
- Cash withdrawal of Leaves is not available. You can use Leaves only by converting them to Sparks.

## 7. Use the Creator Dashboard

Select **Profile → Content Management → Dashboard** to open the **Creator Dashboard**.

- See **Total Messages**, **My Messages**, and **Earned Leaves** at a glance.
- Switch the period between **Today** and **7d** to see the daily trend and top works.
- Select a work under **Performance by Work** to see its details.

The dashboard's message counts are measured differently from the Chat Volume requirement. Check the requirements under **Profile → Creator**.

## 8. Official Creators

When your Chat Volume passes **50,000**, **Apply for Official Creator** becomes available under **Profile → Creator**. Select **Submit Application** to open the application form. Official Creators are selected after a review by our team.

Official Creators differ in these ways:

- The Leaf rate for Original works is 7%.
- Their own chats in their works also earn Leaves.
- They convert 100 Leaves into 100 Sparks.

See the [MYMI Creator Agreement](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/creator-terms) for the full terms.
