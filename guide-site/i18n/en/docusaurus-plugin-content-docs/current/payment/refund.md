# Cancellations and refunds

Where you request a refund depends on **where you paid**. For website purchases, contact MYMI. For app purchases, contact the App Store or Google Play.

| Where you paid | Who to contact |
| --- | --- |
| Website (PayPal) | MYMI ([contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live)) |
| iOS app | Apple (App Store) |
| Android app | Google Play |

## 1. Sparks bought on the website

Website purchases are handled under Article 7 of the [Terms of Service](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/terms-of-service) and PayPal's rules. Sparks are digital service entitlements and are credited to your account as soon as the payment completes.

Contact us in situations such as these:

- A completed payment was not credited
- A major service failure prevents you from using the Sparks you bought
- What you received differs materially from the purchase screen

Whether, how, and when a refund is issued depends on our review, the Terms of Service, and PayPal's rules.

- If any of the purchased Sparks have been used, the entire purchase may be excluded from a refund.
- Free Sparks, event rewards, and Sparks restricted because of a terms violation or unauthorized use may not be refundable.

### How to request

Email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) and include:

- Payment date
- Payment method
- Amount
- Account ID
- PayPal transaction ID

## 2. Refunds for in-app purchases

If you paid in the app, request a refund from your purchase history in the store you used. Refunds follow the store's refund policy and review process, and MYMI cannot decide whether the store approves them.

- **iOS**: See Apple Support's [Request a refund](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223)
- **Android**: See Google Play Help's [refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en)

If the store approves a refund, the Sparks from that purchase may be removed from your account.

## 3. If purchased Sparks do not appear

Check the following before you request a refund or pay again.

1. Refresh the purchase screen on the website, or fully close and reopen the app.
2. Open **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History** and check the **Recharge** tab.
3. If the Sparks still do not appear, email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live).

| Where you paid | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Payment date, payment method, amount, account ID, PayPal transaction ID |
| App | Store (App Store or Google Play), payment date, order number or transaction ID, account ID |

## Getting help

Email payment and refund questions to [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live). For packages and prices, see [Buying Sparks](/payment/payment-methods.md).
