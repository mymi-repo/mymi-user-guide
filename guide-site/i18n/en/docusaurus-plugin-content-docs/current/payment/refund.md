# Cancellations and refunds

Where you ask for a refund depends on **where you paid**.

| Where you paid | Where to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Website (PayPal) | MYMI, by email at [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) |
| iPhone app | Apple (App Store) |
| Android app | Google Play |

If you paid but didn't get your Sparks, go through [these steps](#if-your-sparks-did-not-arrive) before you ask for a refund or pay again.

## If you paid on the website

### When you can get a refund

Website purchases follow the [Purchase and Refund Policy](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/transaction-info-refund-policy) and Section 8 of the [Terms of Service](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/terms-of-service).

Sparks are added to your account right after you pay and can be used right away, so **completed purchases are final** and can't be cancelled or refunded. We do review a refund request in these cases:

- You were charged, but the Sparks weren't added to your account
- A major failure of MYMI kept you from using the Sparks you bought
- What you received is very different from what the purchase screen showed
- You were charged twice, or someone made the purchase without your permission
- A refund is required by law or by PayPal's rules

Whether we refund, and how and when, depends on our review and PayPal's rules. If you've already used some of the Sparks from the purchase, the refund may cover only the unused part. Free Sparks from Attendance Check, events, and Invite Friends can't be refunded.

If the law where you live gives you a right to cancel or get a refund, this guide doesn't take that right away.

### Ask for a refund

1. Email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live).
2. Write your MYMI ID (the `@ID` under your nickname on **Profile**), the payment date and amount, your PayPal transaction ID, and what happened. You can find the transaction ID in PayPal's receipt email or in the details of the payment in your PayPal activity.
3. We review your request and reply by email. If we approve a refund, the money goes back to the payment method you used. How long it takes depends on PayPal and your card issuer.

## If you paid in the app

Refunds for app purchases are requested from the store's purchase history. They follow the store's refund policy and review, and MYMI can't decide whether the store approves a refund.

- **iPhone**: follow Apple Support's [Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).
- **Android**: follow Google Play Help's [refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en).

If the store approves a refund, the Sparks from that purchase can be removed from your account.

## If your Sparks did not arrive

Go through these steps before you pay again.

1. On the website, refresh the purchase screen. In the app, close it completely and open it again. On Android, the app rechecks unfinished payments, and "Previous purchase restored" appears when your Sparks are delivered.
2. Open the **Recharge** tab in **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History** and look for the purchase.
3. If it's still not there, email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) with the details below. On iPhone, if reopening the app doesn't help, don't wait and contact us right away.

| Where you paid | What to send |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Payment date, amount, your MYMI ID, and your PayPal transaction ID |
| App | The store (App Store or Google Play), payment date, order number or transaction ID, and your MYMI ID |

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>I paid in the app. Can I ask MYMI for a refund?</summary>

MYMI can't process refunds for app purchases. Ask in your App Store or Google Play purchase history. If you paid but your Sparks didn't arrive, contact us.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I used some of the Sparks. Can I still get a refund?</summary>

For website purchases, the refund may cover only the Sparks you haven't used. If a problem on MYMI's side kept you from using them properly, email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) and tell us what happened.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My child bought Sparks without my permission</summary>

Minors can't buy Sparks on MYMI. If it was a website purchase, email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) with the payment details. For an app purchase, ask the App Store or Google Play. We handle these cases as the law requires, including any right the law gives you to cancel the purchase.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I opened a dispute with PayPal or my bank</summary>

While a dispute is under review, we may hold the Sparks from that purchase. If the charge is reversed in your favor, those Sparks are removed from your account. If you can, please contact us before you open a dispute.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want a refund before I delete my account</summary>

When you delete your account, your remaining Sparks disappear, so ask before you delete. If you've already deleted it, email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) with the MYMI ID of the deleted account and your payment details.

</details>
