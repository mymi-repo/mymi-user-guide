# Buying Sparks

**Sparks** are what you spend to chat with the characters in a story. Each message uses Sparks based on the AI model you choose and the length of the reply. See the [AI model guide](/chatting/ai-models.md) for each model's cost.

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Spark | Used for chatting. Buy them, or earn them through Attendance Check, Invite Friends, and events |
| Leaf | A reward creators earn when their works are used. Leaves can be converted into Sparks. See [Support creators](/creator-support/support.md) |

## 1. Open the purchase screen

1. On **Profile**, select **My Spark**.
2. In **Spark Management**, select **Charge Sparks**.

You also reach the purchase screen from a notice that you are out of Sparks, or by selecting the balance at the top of the chat room **⋮** menu. You need to sign in to buy Sparks.

## 2. Buy on the website

The website uses **PayPal**, and prices are in US dollars (USD). You can also pay with cards and other methods that PayPal supports.

1. Choose a package on the purchase screen.
2. Check the **Product**, **Total Amount**, and **Before you pay** details (seller, price, payment, delivery, returns and refunds) shown below the packages.
3. If you have not confirmed your age yet, select **Verify Age** and confirm that you are 18 or older.
4. Select the PayPal button that appears and complete the payment on the PayPal screen.
5. Return to MYMI and check the confirmation message.

| Price | Sparks | Notes |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| $3.49 | 5,000 | |
| $6.99 | 10,000 | |
| $20.99 | 31,500 | Includes a 1,500 bonus |
| $34.99 | 55,000 | Includes a 5,000 bonus |
| $69.99 | 115,000 | Includes a 15,000 bonus |

Exchange rates and cross-border fees follow the terms of your payment method.

:::info[Gemini Pass sales ended]
Gemini Pass sales ended on June 22, 2026. Only Spark packages are sold now.
:::

## 3. Buy in the app

The app uses in-app purchases through the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android).

1. Choose a package on the purchase screen and select **Purchase**.
2. Complete the payment in the store's payment sheet.
3. Check the confirmation message.

App prices are set in each store and differ from website prices. Check the amount on the purchase screen and in the store's payment sheet before you pay.

The app also offers a one-time **First Buy** package of 5,000 Sparks. It is available only to accounts that have completed [phone verification](/account/adult-verification.md), and only once per account.

## 4. Earn free Sparks

### Attendance Check

Go to **Profile → Attendance Check** and select **Check-in and get Sparks** to receive Sparks once a day. Your account needs to have completed phone verification.

| Streak | Sparks |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–9 | Starts at 200 and goes up by 20 each day (200, 220, 240, 260, 280, 300, 320, 340, 360) |
| Day 10 | 400 |

- A full 10-day streak earns 2,920 Sparks. On day 11, the rewards start again from day 1.
- If you miss a day, your streak starts over from day 1.
- The day changes at 15:00 UTC (for example, 11:00 a.m. Eastern Daylight Time).

### Invite Friends

Go to **Profile → Invite Friends** and send your friend **My referral code**, or select **Share with friends** to send an invite link.

1. Your friend completes phone verification and registers the referral code **within 7 days** of signing up. If they signed up through your invite link, the code is registered automatically when they complete phone verification.
2. If your friend makes their first paid purchase **within 30 days** of registering the code, you and your friend each receive **1,500 Sparks**.

Registering a code without a purchase does not earn the reward. You cannot register your own code.

### Events

You may also receive Sparks from events or contests. Check each event announcement for its conditions.

## 5. Check expiration dates

How long you can use Sparks depends on how you received them. Expired Sparks disappear automatically. When you chat, **Sparks that expire soonest are used first**.

| How you received them | Available for |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase, Leaf conversion | 1 year from receipt |
| Attendance Check, events | 14 days from receipt |
| Invite Friends reward | 30 days from receipt |
| Contest reward | 180 days from receipt |

**Spark Management** shows **Paid Spark** and **Free Spark** separately. If any Sparks expire within 24 hours, you will see ⏰ with "expiring soon."

## 6. View your Spark history

Select **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History** to open **Spark Usage History**.

- **Usage** tab: Sparks you spent on chats and Sparks that expired
- **Recharge** tab: Sparks from purchases, Attendance Check, Invite Friends, and Leaf conversion. Sparks returned after a failed reply also appear here

## If a purchase does not go through

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The PayPal button does not appear | Check that you have completed age confirmation. If you see **Verify Age**, confirm your age first |
| I cannot buy the First Buy package | It is available only in the app. Check that you have completed phone verification and have not bought it before |
| I paid, but my Sparks did not increase | Refresh the purchase screen on the website, or restart the app. If they still do not appear, do not pay again. Contact us as described in [Cancellations and refunds](/payment/refund.md) |
| The payment screen does not open, or I am not returned to MYMI | If you opened MYMI inside another app, try the browser you normally use |
| App and website prices differ | App purchases are charged at the price set in the store |
| I cannot claim my Attendance Check reward | Check that you are signed in and have completed phone verification. You can claim once a day |
| I did not receive the Invite Friends reward | Check that your friend registered the code within 7 days of signing up and made their first paid purchase within 30 days of registering it |

## Getting help

Email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) with payment questions. Include the payment date, payment method, amount, account ID, and PayPal transaction ID (for app purchases, the store and order number) to help us check quickly.
