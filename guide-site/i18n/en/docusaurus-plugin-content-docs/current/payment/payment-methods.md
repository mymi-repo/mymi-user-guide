# Buying Sparks

**Sparks** are what you spend to get AI replies on MYMI. Each time you get a reply, the price of the AI model you chose is deducted. You can see each model's price in the [AI model guide](/chatting/ai-models.md).

If you run low, you can buy more, or get free Sparks from Attendance Check and Invite Friends.

## Open the purchase screen

1. Log in, then select **My Spark** on **Profile**.
2. In **Spark Management**, select **Charge Sparks**.

While chatting, you can also select **Charge** in the notice that says you're out of Sparks, or select your Spark balance at the top of the chat room's ⋮ menu.

## Buy on the website

On the website, you pay with **PayPal** in US dollars (USD). PayPal also accepts cards and other payment methods it supports.

1. Choose a package on the purchase screen.
2. Check the **Product** and **Total Amount**, then read the **Before you pay** box below them. It shows the seller, the price, when you are charged, when the Sparks arrive, and the refund terms.
3. If you haven't confirmed your age yet, select **Verify Age** and complete [age confirmation](/account/adult-verification.md#confirm-your-age). You need to be 18 or older to buy Sparks.
4. Select the PayPal button that appears, and finish paying on the PayPal screen.
5. Return to MYMI and check that the charge is complete.

## Buy in the app

In the app, you pay through the **App Store** on iPhone or **Google Play** on Android.

1. Choose a package on the purchase screen and select **Purchase**.
2. Finish paying in the store's payment sheet.
3. Check that the charge is complete.

App prices are set by each store and shown in your local currency, so they differ from the website's. Check the amount on the purchase screen and in the store's payment sheet before you pay.

The app also has a one-time **First Buy** package of 5,000 Sparks. In the app, only accounts that have completed [phone verification](/account/adult-verification.md) can buy it. Each account can buy it once across the website and the app combined, so if you've already bought the $1.99 package on the website, you can't buy it in the app.

## Get free Sparks

### Attendance Check

Select **Check-in and get Sparks** in **Profile → Attendance Check** to get Sparks once a day. Only accounts that have completed phone verification can receive them. If you haven't verified yet, a verification window opens when you select the button.

| Streak | Sparks |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–9 | Starts at 200 and goes up by 20 each day (200, 220, 240, 260, 280, 300, 320, 340, 360) |
| Day 10 | 400 |

- A full 10-day streak earns 2,920 Sparks, and the next day starts again from day 1.
- If you skip a day, your streak starts again from day 1.
- The day changes at 15:00 UTC. That is 11:00 a.m. Eastern or 8:00 a.m. Pacific during daylight saving time, and one hour earlier the rest of the year.

### Invite Friends

1. On **Profile → Invite Friends**, copy **My referral code** and send it to your friend, or select **Share with friends** to send an invite link.
2. Your friend signs up and completes phone verification **within 7 days**. Then they enter the code under **Enter referral code** in their own **Profile → Invite Friends** and select **Register referral code**. If they signed up through your invite link, the code is registered automatically when they finish phone verification.
3. If your friend makes their first paid purchase **within 30 days** of the day they register the code, you and your friend each get **1,500 Sparks**.

:::info[It has to be your friend's first purchase on MYMI]
The reward is given only for your friend's first paid purchase. If your friend has ever bought Sparks on the website or in the app, neither of you gets the reward. You can't register your own code.
:::

### Events

You may also get Sparks from events or contests. Check each event's announcement for its conditions and how long the Sparks last.

## When do Sparks expire?

How long you can use Sparks depends on how you got them. Expired Sparks disappear automatically. When you chat, **the Sparks that expire soonest are used first**.

| How you got them | How long they last |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase, Leaf conversion | 1 year from the day you got them |
| Attendance Check | 14 days from the day you got them |
| Invite Friends reward | 30 days from the day you got them |
| Events | The period in the announcement (14 days if none is given) |
| Contest rewards | 180 days from the day you got them |

Select **Profile → My Spark** to see **Paid Spark** and **Free Spark** separately in **Spark Management**. If some Sparks will expire within 24 hours, you see a note such as "⏰ 300 expiring soon".

## See your history

Select **Profile → My Spark → View Usage History** to open **Spark Usage History**.

- **Usage** tab: Sparks you spent in chats, and Sparks that expired
- **Recharge** tab: Sparks you got from purchases, Attendance Check, Invite Friends, and Leaf conversion. Sparks returned because a reply couldn't be made also show up here

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>I can't buy the First Buy package</summary>

- On the website, the First Buy package needs only age confirmation. In the app, if a window titled **Identity Verification Required** appears, select **Verify Identity** and finish [phone verification](/account/adult-verification.md) first.
- If you see "You have already used this promotional plan." or "This promotion has already been used.", you have already bought it. Each account can buy it once, across the website and the app combined.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I paid, but the Sparks didn't arrive</summary>

Don't pay again. On the website, refresh the purchase screen. In the app, close it completely and open it again. If the Sparks still aren't there, follow [If your Sparks did not arrive](/payment/refund.md#if-your-sparks-did-not-arrive) to contact us.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says "Payment was cancelled."</summary>

This appears if you close the payment window before you finish. Start again from choosing a package. If you got a payment notice from PayPal or your bank anyway, follow [If your Sparks did not arrive](/payment/refund.md#if-your-sparks-did-not-arrive).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The PayPal button doesn't appear</summary>

If you see **Verify Age** where the PayPal button should be, you haven't confirmed your age yet. Select it and complete age confirmation, and the PayPal button appears. If the button still doesn't show, refresh the page and choose the package again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The payment window won't open, or I'm not sent back to MYMI</summary>

If you opened MYMI inside another app such as Instagram or Facebook, open [www.mymi.live](https://www.mymi.live) in Chrome or Safari and try again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The prices in the app and on the website are different</summary>

In the app, you pay the price set by the store, so it differs from the website. The website and the app share one account, so you can use Sparks you bought in either place.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't get my Attendance Check reward</summary>

- Only accounts that have completed phone verification can receive it. When you select **Check-in and get Sparks**, a verification window opens.
- If you see "Already checked in today.", you already got today's reward. You can get the next one after the day changes at 15:00 UTC.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My Invite Friends reward didn't arrive</summary>

- Check that your friend completed phone verification and registered the code within 7 days of signing up. If you see "The referral code entry window has expired.", the 7 days have passed.
- Your friend's first paid purchase has to come within 30 days of registering the code. If your friend bought Sparks before registering the code, the reward isn't given.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My Sparks went down</summary>

Expired Sparks disappear automatically. In the **Usage** tab of **Spark Usage History**, you can see the Sparks you spent in chats and the Sparks that expired. See [When do Sparks expire?](#when-do-sparks-expire) for how long each kind lasts.

</details>

For payment questions, email [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live). Please include the payment date, payment method, amount, your MYMI ID (the `@ID` under your nickname on **Profile**), and your PayPal transaction ID so we can check faster.
