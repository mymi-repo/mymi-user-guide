## What you must not do

These are the main prohibitions in the [Work Creation Policy](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/content-creation-policy) and the [Terms of Service](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/terms-of-service).

- **Content involving minors**: Sexual content about anyone under 18, or about a character who looks under 18. This is banned in every context
- **Real people**: Sexualizing, mocking, or impersonating real people, and making or sharing intimate images of a real person without consent, including AI-generated "deepfake" images
- **Illegal content**: Making or spreading content that breaks the law
- **Hate speech**: Expressions that discriminate against or demean people based on race, gender, religion, and similar traits
- **Privacy violations**: Using or sharing someone else's personal information without permission
- **Rights infringement**: Using someone else's work or likeness without permission
- **Wrong rating**: Registering a work with adult content as a general work
- **Using generated images outside MYMI**: Downloading or capturing images made on MYMI to post or sell them outside MYMI
- **Getting around safety measures**: Dodging age checks, safety filters, or usage limits, or using bots, automation, or mass generation

## What happens if you break the rules

Depending on the type and number of violations, you can receive a warning, have works unpublished or deleted, have features limited, be suspended from the service, or be permanently suspended.

- Sexual content involving minors is deleted at the first violation, and the account is permanently suspended.
- Other serious violations can lead to permanent suspension no matter how many times they happen.
- The sanction table in the [Work Creation Policy](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/content-creation-policy) lists the sanctions for each type.
- If your account is closed for breaking the Terms, your unused Sparks and Leaves are lost, unless the law requires otherwise.

:::warning[Content can be removed without notice]
Works or content that break the policies can be deleted or made private without warning.
:::

## Report a work or a user

1. Open the detail screen of the work you want to report.
2. Select the shield icon at the top right, then **Report**. You need to log in to report.
3. Choose a reason, add details if you need to, and select **Submit report**.

We aim to make a first check of each report within 24 hours and to act within 72 hours. In the same menu, select **Block creator** or **Block content** and that creator or work stops showing up on your Home and in recommendations.

You can also report or ask by email.

| When | Send to |
| --- | --- |
| You want to report harmful content | [safety@mymi.live](mailto:safety@mymi.live) |
| Someone used your work or likeness without permission | [copyright@mymi.live](mailto:copyright@mymi.live) |
| You disagree with a sanction you received | [safety_appeal@mymi.live](mailto:safety_appeal@mymi.live) |
| You have a question about your personal information | [privacy@mymi.live](mailto:privacy@mymi.live) |
| Any other question | [contact@mymi.live](mailto:contact@mymi.live) |

### Ask us to remove an intimate image of you

If an intimate image or video of you, including an AI-generated or edited one, was posted on MYMI without your consent, email [safety@mymi.live](mailto:safety@mymi.live). Include your physical or electronic signature, a link to the content, a short statement that you believe in good faith it was posted without your consent, and how to reach you. We remove the content as soon as possible and no later than 48 hours after we receive a valid request, and we look for identical copies. The details are in the [Youth Protection and Safety Policy](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/youth-protection-policy).

### Send a copyright notice

If someone used your copyrighted work on MYMI without permission, follow the steps in Section 10 of the [Terms of Service](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/terms-of-service) and send your notice to [copyright@mymi.live](mailto:copyright@mymi.live).

## If you get stuck

<details>
<summary>My work was suddenly unpublished or deleted</summary>

A work that is judged to break the Work Creation Policy can be unpublished or deleted without notice. If you disagree, email [safety_appeal@mymi.live](mailto:safety_appeal@mymi.live) with the work's title and your MYMI ID. For a work that was rejected in review, see [Publish and edit](/create/making/publish.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want to see a work or creator I blocked again</summary>

Unblock them in **Profile → Block Management**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't find the Report menu</summary>

Select the shield icon at the top right of the work's detail screen to open the menu. If you're not logged in, a login window appears first when you select Report.

</details>
