## Community guidelines

MYMI reviews works and content under the Work Creation Policy and community guidelines.

### Main prohibitions

- **Illegal content**: Creating or sharing content that violates the law
- **Hate speech**: Discriminatory expressions based on race, gender, religion, and similar characteristics
- **Privacy violations**: Using or disclosing another person's personal information without permission
- **Rights infringement**: Using another person's works or likeness without permission
- **Sexual content involving minors**: Sexual depictions of minors or characters who appear to be minors (strictly prohibited)

### Sanctions

Depending on the type of violation and how many times it has occurred, MYMI may issue a warning, unpublish or delete content, restrict features, suspend the service, or permanently suspend the account. Serious violations, such as sexual content involving minors, can lead to permanent suspension on the first violation. See the sanction table in the [Work Creation Policy](https://www.mymi.live/en/legal/content-creation-policy).

:::danger
Works or content that violate the policies may be removed without prior notice.
:::
